Welcome to my academic blog! I'll be sharing thoughts on reinforcement learning, machine learning research, and my journey as a Ph.D. student at The University of Hong Kong.

## What to Expect

I plan to write about:

- **Research insights** — Key takeaways from papers I read and projects I work on
- **Technical tutorials** — Practical guides on RL algorithms, Bayesian inference, and LLM fine-tuning
- **Academic life** — Reflections on the Ph.D. experience, conferences, and collaborations

## Why Blog?

Writing is thinking. By articulating research ideas in a blog format, I aim to:

1. Deepen my understanding of complex topics
2. Share knowledge with the broader community
3. Document my research journey for future reference

Stay tuned for more posts!
